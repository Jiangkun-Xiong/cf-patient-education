01 / COURSEWORK CONTEXT

Medical learning,
made easier to find.

I created this website during Year D of the CQMU–University of Leicester joint programme, while studying BSc (Hons) Clinical Sciences in the UK in the 2025–2026 academic year.

The original website identifies the course as Respiratory Module. The coursework takes the form of a patient information website about cystic fibrosis, bringing together disease explanations, management topics, educational media and references.

The repository records the coursework background, the original site and the next steps towards a website developed with code.
